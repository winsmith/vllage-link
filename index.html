The people who matter

vllage is a personal relationship manager for Mac and iPhone

It remembers who you know, how you know them and what's coming up

That makes it easier to stay in touch with family, friends and colleagues

> vllage is in active development

> It will be free when it launches

Your phone helps you remember

Your contacts app stores phone numbers and addresses

vllage also stores the context, such as who introduced you and whose child just started school

It reminds you of birthdays, anniversaries and promised calls before they are due

Keep it in mind

Some features ship at launch and the rest follow in updates

  • Write down what you know

    Each person has a name, nickname, employer and job title

    Free-form notes hold everything else, like a pet's name or the book you promised to lend

  • See how everyone is connected

    Relationships include colleague, partner, mentor, family and the person who introduced you

    Each relationship reads correctly from both sides

    A map, groups, tags and search help you find people

  • Never miss a date

    vllage reminds you of birthdays and anniversaries a few days ahead

    Todos have due dates and reminders

    A digest every Monday morning lists the week ahead

  • Keep in touch

    A log records when you last spoke to someone

    You can set how often you want to be in touch

    Each person links to their Apple Contacts card and their LinkedIn, Mastodon, Bluesky or GitHub profile

  • Bring your people with you

    vllage imports vCard and CSV files, including the LinkedIn connections export

    A preview shows likely duplicates before anything is saved

  • On your Mac and iPhone

    Your data syncs between your devices and stays readable offline

    A browser version, widgets, Shortcuts and Spotlight search are planned

Your data is yours alone

The vllage server only syncs your data between your devices

Nothing you write is shared with other users

Email addresses and phone numbers from Apple Contacts reach the server only as hashes

You can export all your data and delete your account at any time

vllage will never

  • share your people, or anything about them, with another user
  • message anyone on your behalf
  • scrape LinkedIn, Mastodon, Bluesky or anywhere else
  • turn your friends into a sales pipeline

vllage will be free

The Mac and iPhone apps will be free to download

A package to run the sync server on your own hardware is planned
